2 Experiences

Put Teaching Experience first when you have it. Keep the rest broad: Related Experience, Leadership, Campus Involvement, Volunteer Experience, or Other Work.

Change your section order

Teaching Experience stays first when it is included. Move the other broad sections up or down based on what best supports the teaching role. Experiences within each section are automatically arranged by most recent end date.

3 Professional Prep

Add professional development and certifications

This step is optional. Use it for preparation outside your degree record—certifications, credentials, workshops, conferences, district or school training, curriculum training, classroom management training, literacy/math training, or other relevant professional learning.

Keep this separate from experience. A CPR certification or literacy workshop is professional preparation; student teaching, tutoring, coaching, or work with youth belongs in an experience section.
4 Skills

Add technical or specialty skills

This step is optional. Include only specific technical, content-area, equipment, software, or language skills that add something meaningful for the role—not communication, teamwork, classroom management, differentiation, or other strengths that should be demonstrated through experience bullets.
