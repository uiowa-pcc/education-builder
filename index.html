2 Experience Details

Set up your experiences

Add the basic details for every experience you may include. Put Teaching Experience first when you have it, but keep non-teaching experiences when they show relevant transferable skills, responsibility, leadership, service, or subject-area expertise.

Change your section order

Teaching Experience stays first when it is included. Move the other broad sections up or down based on what best supports the teaching role. Experiences within each section are automatically arranged by most recent end date.

3 Bullets

Add bullets to your experiences

Work on one experience at a time. All of your experiences stay visible below, but only one opens at a time so you can focus on the strongest evidence for that experience.

Use the same formula throughout the suite. Power verb + what you did + one useful detail about who/scope, how, or why/result. Teaching and non-teaching experiences can both demonstrate strong transferable skills.
4 Professional Preparation

Add professional development and certifications

Keep these separate from your experience sections. Add only items that support your teaching preparation or qualifications.

Professional Development

Workshops, conferences, district or school training, curriculum training, Baker Teacher Leader Center opportunities, and other professional learning.

Certifications

Credentials such as CPR / First Aid, substitute authorization, technology certifications, or other relevant certifications.

5 Skills

Add technical or specialty skills

This step is optional. Include only specific technical, content-area, equipment, software, or language skills that add something meaningful for the role—not communication, teamwork, classroom management, differentiation, or other strengths that should be demonstrated through experience bullets.
